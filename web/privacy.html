Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Data We Collect

When you sign in with Apple, we receive your Apple-provided user ID, display name, and email address (unless you choose to hide it, in which case we never see it).

When you are signed in, your cellar (the wines, counts, prices and notes you enter) is backed up to Firebase Firestore under your user ID so it can be restored on your other devices.

If you enable push notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging receives a device token to deliver them. We do not store this token ourselves.

Data We Do Not Collect

Location data Analytics Usage tracking Advertising identifiers

Local Storage

Display preferences (dark mode, filters) stay on your device. Your cellar is also saved on your device; it is only copied to Firebase Firestore while you are signed in.

Third-Party Services

Firebase Authentication | Handles sign-in (Google Privacy Policy)
Firebase Firestore | Backs up your cellar while you are signed in
Firebase Cloud Messaging | Delivers push notifications

Data Deletion

You can delete your account directly from the app. Open the Profile tab, scroll down, and tap Delete Account. You will be asked to re-authenticate with Apple before deletion completes. This permanently removes your sign-in credentials, your cellar backup, and any tasting notes you posted. The copy of your cellar stored on your device stays there until you clear it from the Cellar tab.

Third-Party Links

The app may link to external websites (e.g. Vivino, Systembolaget). Those sites have their own privacy policies and we have no control over their content or practices.
