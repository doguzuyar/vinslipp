Delete Your Account

Open the app and go to the Profile tab. Scroll down and tap Delete Account. You will be asked to re-authenticate with Apple and confirm before deletion completes.

Deletion permanently removes your sign-in credentials, your cellar backup, and any tasting notes you posted. The cellar saved on your device stays until you clear it. This cannot be undone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is a wine missing from the releases list?The list is generated daily from Systembolaget's API. If a wine was added or updated after the last sync, it will appear the following day.
How do I import my Vivino cellar?Export your data from Vivino (Account → Privacy → Download my data), then open the Profile tab in Vinslipp and tap Import Vivino data.
Push notifications are not arriving.Make sure notifications are enabled for Vinslipp in iOS Settings. Then open the Profile tab, tap Notifications, and select your preferred wine category. If the issue persists, try toggling the setting off and back on.
The auction data looks outdated.Bukowskis auction statistics are updated after each auction closes. The next scheduled update will appear automatically.
